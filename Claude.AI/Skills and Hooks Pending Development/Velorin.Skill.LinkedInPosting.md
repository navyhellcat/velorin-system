## SEE MORE CUTOFF — EXACT NUMBERS
- Mobile truncation: ~140 characters
- Desktop truncation: ~210 characters
- Real-world range from direct testing: 120–210 characters depending on line breaks and media
- Two consecutive newlines (paragraph break) end the snippet early — keep hook in a single paragraph
- Community data (5,000+ posts): -20% performance when hook is longer than 1 line
- Hook formatting: [LINE - GAP - LINE] or [LINE - LINE - GAP] before see more

## HOOK RULES
- 1 sentence, under 140 characters, no paragraph break before cutoff
- Hook's only job: earn the next 5 seconds of attention
- Four types that perform: contrarian statement, specific number, pain point confession, unexpected comparison
- Kill words: "I'm excited to share," "Happy Monday," "Thoughts?" — all invisible
- Draft 3–7 variations, pick the strongest — the first idea is rarely it

## ALGORITHM — HOW IT WORKS (2026)
### Three phases:
1. Spam filter → pass/fail immediately on publish
2. Shown to 2–5% of network in first 30–60 minutes → engagement quality scored
3. Strong performers expand to 2nd/3rd degree + hashtag followers + interest groups

### Primary metric: Depth Score
- Dwell time (primary): 61+ seconds = 15.6% engagement rate vs 1.2% at 0–3 seconds
- Comment depth: substantive threads > single replies > generic "Great post!" (flagged as low value)
- Saves: strongest quality signal — structure content as frameworks/checklists to drive saves
- Shares with context
- Reactions: least weighted

### First hour is destiny
- Only 5% of underperforming posts in hour 1 recover to broad reach
- Reply to every comment in first 60 minutes
- Strong early engagement triggers 2nd/3rd degree expansion

## WHAT KILLS REACH
- External links in post body: -60% reach
- Link in first comment: also penalized as of early 2026
- Engagement bait ("Comment YES if you agree"): actively suppressed
- Engagement pods: shadowbanned — AI detects reciprocal patterns
- AI-sounding phrasing: "In today's fast-paced digital landscape," "Delve into," "Unlock the power of" — downranked for low perplexity
- Over 5 hashtags: diminishing returns
- Generic "thoughts?" CTA: generates low-value comments, hurts depth score

## FORMAT PERFORMANCE (engagement rate)
| Format | Avg Engagement Rate |
|---|---|
| PDF Carousel (document) | 6.60% |
| Native Video | 5.60% |
| Text-only | ~2.0% |
| Single image | ~1.4% (underperforms text by 30%) |
| External link post | -60% reach penalty |
| Poll | Comments but lower reach |

- Carousels: 8–12 slides, one idea per slide, strong hook on slide 1, CTA on last slide
- Video: under 30 seconds = 200% higher completion rate; captions mandatory; native upload only (no YouTube links)
- Portrait images (1200x1500px): 62% better than landscape on mobile
- Personal profiles: 13x more reach than company pages

## POST LENGTH
- Avoid under 400 characters — underperforms across all metrics
- Sweet spot for text posts: 1,300–2,000 characters
- Max is 3,000 characters (~430 words)
- Tighter almost always wins over maxing the limit

## FORMATTING RULES
- White space and short paragraphs — mobile first
- Minimal Unicode bold — 1 phrase max, not paragraphs (screen reader issue)
- Emojis: 1–2 for structure, not decoration
- 1–3 niche hashtags at the end (hashtag impact weakened significantly in 2025–2026)
- No wall of text, no checkmark every line, no AI template skeleton
- Over-formatting reads as generated — readers scroll past it

## TIMING (consensus: 4 studies, 8M+ posts, 2B+ engagements)
- Best window: Tuesday–Thursday, 10 AM–noon (audience timezone)
- Single strongest slot: Wednesday 4 PM
- Emerging window: 3–8 PM weekdays now outperforms mornings (2026 shift)
- Worst: weekends, Monday morning, Friday afternoon
- Post frequency sweet spot: 3–5x/week; 1 quality post beats 5 filler posts

### If posting N times per week:
- 1x → Tuesday morning
- 2x → Tuesday + Wednesday
- 3x → Tuesday + Wednesday + Thursday

## PROFILE ALIGNMENT AFFECTS POST REACH
- Vague headline ("Marketing Professional") → reduced distribution
- Optimized headline ("B2B SaaS | Air Filtration Sales | AI Integration") → better distribution
- LinkedIn uses profile topic DNA to match posts to interest graph audiences

## QUICK CHECKLIST
1. Hook = 1 sentence, under 140 chars, no line break before see more
2. No external links anywhere in post or first comment
3. Body = 1,300–2,000 characters
4. End with specific question requiring a real answer (not "thoughts?")
5. Reply to every comment in hour 1
6. White space formatting, short paragraphs, minimal bold
7. 1–3 niche hashtags at end
8. Post Tue–Thu, 10 AM–noon or 3–5 PM audience timezone
9. Best format for reach: PDF carousel 8–12 slides
10. No engagement bait, no pods, no AI phrasing
